# Phase 8A baseline audit

Audited October 12, 2026 through Graphify query, source inspection, and a read-only Unity MCP command. Unity 6000.3.26f1 was open on `Assets/Town/Scenes/DaylightTown.unity`, not in Play Mode; the scene was clean. Exactly one NetworkManager was present. The original eight-page GDD and the expanded reference research were read. No updated GDD v2 was located by repository filename search.

The pre-8A baseline is preserved at `D:/Business as Usual Backups/pre-phase8A-2026-10-12`, including editable source copies, file hashes, working-tree status, and the index tree. No staging, commit, push, reset or discard was authorized or performed.

| Area | Verified baseline | Consequence / priority |
|---|---|---|
| Networking | `NetworkSession` approves at most two clients. `NetworkPlayer` sends owner-authenticated server RPCs; the server moves bodies and owns work. `NetworkShop` validates sequence, global revision, range, line of sight and permissions. | Four-player capacity and spawn separation are blocking changes; preserve this authority boundary. Client movement has no prediction. |

| Sanitation | Six indexed bags, known material, hold-E sorting, manifest and close. Repair/delivery makes extra bags available. | Route choice exists but material judgment is shallow. Preserve recovery; add packaging and scrap with IDs tied to the repair. |
| Records | Complete old stock journals and old service/disposal arrays are included in shared snapshots; handbook renders profession-specific copies. | UI-only filtering is insufficient for future evidence. New workday records must use targeted RPCs, and its internal audit must not be serialized into broadcasts. No secret information is implemented in this phase. |
| Market entry | Door and portal are aligned at x≈−8.2; portal is a separate E target at z≈4.2. Interior is offset at x≈−40. Prioritize an automatic walking threshold retaining the connected room and multiplayer state. No scene restructure needed. |
| Environment | Existing market/workshop shells, fitted wall/prop collisions, service vehicles, yard bins, street/square and minimap are present. No warehouse/home interior is playable. | Reuse workplace footprints. Every new solid presentation prop needs a fitted collider; work triggers need reachable standing space. |

Staff selection stretches the book artwork behind wide repeated rows. | Replace selection backing with proportional paper/card primitives, keep icons and fonts, and verify resolution screenshots. | Prior validation | Phase 7B documents two-process automated checks and native rendering evidence. | Historical reports do not prove the new four-player workday or enjoyment. Rerun relevant compatibility suites and new four-process checks. |

Remaining risks to inspect manually: first-person movement latency over LAN, procedural wrist/tool poses, readability at narrow displays, the short threshold transition, colleague congestion, long-session performance, and whether choices have enough consequence. The warm/flat lighting and model consistency remain human-review items; no new environment generation is needed to test this workday.

New workday runtime mode replaces the old town task prompts within the same authored scene. Historic opt-in harness runs without `--bau-workday` retain the earlier sequences for compatibility testing. This is a test-mode distinction, not two concurrent inventories for a profession: the active workday has one shared physical registry, and the legacy sequences are not active sources for it.
