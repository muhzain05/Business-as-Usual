# Make ordinary work generate extraordinary suspicion

**Business as Usual should build its mystery out of useful work, shared objects, and incomplete perspectives.** The strongest transferable lesson across these references is that an observable action becomes interesting when its purpose remains uncertain and another player can choose how to investigate it. A criminal who actually repairs a vehicle, supplies a colleague, or helps reload a cart can earn believable trust while diverting a package elsewhere. Innocents need small private motives that explain suspicious conduct without making everyone an interchangeable saboteur. A compact town should create overlapping errands and alternative routes; its records should narrow accounts without classifying people. For Phase 8, prioritize one integrated parcel–part–waste loop over separately polishing every profession. Test a four-player, one-day work-and-deduction slice before expanding to the original three-day MVP or six-player, five-day vision.

Research date: October 12, 2026. **Verified** identifies mechanics supported by linked primary rules, developer descriptions, or first-person designer interviews. **Interpretation** identifies the reasoning about their likely experiential value; this is not measured evidence that a mechanic causes fun. **BAU proposal** identifies new designs, including everything in the opportunities, ranking, scenarios, and prototype sections. No four-person human session or new Unity runtime inspection was performed for this report.

## Seventeen references offer different forms of uncertainty

Each comparison explicitly considers decisions, information held and missing, alibis, cooperation/betrayal, unexpected situations, repetition, environment, and adaptation. Some references have no human alibi or betrayal system. That absence matters: an authored mystery supplies evidence-design lessons, not a solution to multiplayer replayability.

### Dale & Dawson makes lawful office activity look questionable

**Verified:** Specialists do workplace tasks, Slackers imitate productivity, and a Manager observes and fires employees; proximity voice supports discussion. A September 2024 developer announcement described a camera-firmware task that temporarily disables surveillance and can also be exploited by Slackers. Treat this as a documented version-specific example, not a guarantee of the current task pool. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2920570/Dale__Dawson_Stationery_Supplies/), [Developer announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2920570/announcements/))

**Interpretation:** The decision is how to allocate attention between work and appearing trustworthy. Players know their assignment and nearby actions; colleagues' purposes and offscreen activity remain hidden. A work errand creates an alibi, challenged by asking what it accomplished and who saw it. Productivity enables cooperation; diverted attention permits deception. A legitimate break or maintenance task can unexpectedly resemble misconduct. Different assignments, encounters, and managerial judgments change the social story, although familiarity can still produce repetitive scrutiny. Rooms and workstations restrict who observes whom.

**BAU proposal:** Every covert interaction needs a useful lawful counterpart. Returning stock and correcting records should be suspicious only in context. Replace performance-of-work deception with **genuine criminal productivity**; do not import managerial firing.

### LOCKDOWN Protocol puts observation in competition with deadlines

**Verified:** The developer describes first-person real-time communication and cooperation to finish tasks before time expires, opposed by hidden dissidents trying to prevent completion undetected. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2780980/LOCKDOWN_Protocol/))

**Interpretation:** Players decide whether to continue work, coordinate a bottleneck, or spend time watching someone. They know their alignment, current task context, and nearby events; another worker's reason for delaying progress remains incomplete. A visible contribution or claimed errand supplies an alibi, tested against local witnesses and outcomes. The deadline makes help valuable and gives obstruction leverage. Several people attempting the same practical solution can create unscripted interference. Timing, movement, and communication change encounters; the retrieved source does not establish specific randomized tasks. Spatial separation distributes observations.

**BAU proposal:** Give an ordinary delivery a manageable deadline and allow independent work during supply waits. A stalled job needs several plausible causes. Take the pressure to cooperate; do not make stopping all town work the criminal's main goal. Specific physics comedy and detailed task manipulations were not verified here and remain prototype ideas.

### Project Winter makes trust useful before betrayal becomes costly

**Verified:** Survivors gather supplies, repair escape objectives, explore buildings, and face weather/wildlife. Traitors know teammates and have private communication and resources. Current official descriptions distinguish modes and describe blizzards prompting regrouping. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/774861/Project_Winter/))

**Interpretation:** Traveling together offers reassurance but reduces collection efficiency. Each survivor knows local resources and actions; traitors possess additional team knowledge. Distant expeditions and supply provenance remain uncertain. Gathering or exploration claims create alibis, challenged through stock, route, and companion accounts. Shared survival needs make assistance meaningful before betrayal. Environmental pressure can interrupt a secret plan or explain an honest delay. Group composition, timing, and pressure recombine; exact procedural generation is not established by this source. Distributed buildings and exposure make movement consequential.

**BAU proposal:** An urgent garage request and a shared stock delivery create dependence without survival punishment. Regroup around work rather than combat or blizzards. A delayed return should invite corroboration, not a sabotage notification; criminal-only hatches would undermine legitimate access ambiguity.

### SpyParty turns purpose and attention into a contest

**Verified:** A spy completes missions while blending into party guests; a sniper observes. Developer materials distinguish action tells from suspicious behavior, explain occlusion and cover, and discuss selected mission subsets preventing observation of one mandatory action. ([Developer site](https://www.spyparty.com/), [Developer manual](https://shared.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/329070/manuals/SpyParty-Manual-screen.pdf?t=1726041201), [Mission-subsetting discussion](https://www.spyparty.com/page/23/?orderemail=&payment=))

**Interpretation:** The spy chooses progress versus exposure; the observer chooses where attention is worth spending. The spy knows identity/objectives, while the observer knows possible actions and witnessed behavior. Obstruction and divided attention leave gaps. Alibis are primarily mundane behavioral cover, challenged by repeated purposeful movement and changed objects. The baseline is competitive rather than cooperative. Adaptive opponents create unscripted confrontations; changing mission subsets and observation habits support repetition, although learned tells can dominate. Sightlines, crowding, and object placement govern opportunity.

**BAU proposal:** Opening a cabinet must serve both legitimate inspection and covert handling. Let observers miss context rather than see a unique criminal animation. Never use one-shot execution, arrest, or a universal visual guilt tell. The historical manual verifies principles, not every current mission implementation.

### Deception requires an explanation rather than a suspicious name

**Verified:** In Deception: Murder in Hong Kong, a murderer selects a means and key evidence. An informed forensic scientist communicates through constrained scene clues; investigators interpret while the murderer misdirects. Identifying a person alone is insufficient. Optional witness/accomplice roles begin at larger player counts. ([Publisher explanation](https://greyfoxgames.com/collections/current-games/products/deception-murder-in-hong-kong), [Official English rules](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0669/3961/7510/files/deception_rulebook_english.pdf?v=1699426602))

**Interpretation:** Players choose which combination explains an ambiguous clue and which argument to challenge. The scientist has the answer, the murderer knows the selected objects, and investigators share clues without knowing allegiance. Interpretation and persuasion are unreliable; spatial alibis are not the main mechanism. Cooperative reasoning gives the murderer a conversation to redirect. Unscripted arguments surround a selected crime rather than a simulated incident. Dealt combinations and group vocabulary vary rounds. The displayed evidence and restricted clue vocabulary form the environment.

**BAU proposal:** Closing discussion should ask what happened to which package, through which opportunity, and what supports that account. Avoid a designated omniscient scientist or correct-answer oracle. The town itself must generate partial observations.

### Obra Dinn organizes fragments into a revisable account

**Verified:** Return of the Obra Dinn presents an insurance investigation through exploration and frozen death scenes. Lucas Pope describes using a book to organize identities, fates, and events encountered out of order. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/653530/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn/), [First-person developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-lucas-pope-s-i-return-of-the-obra-dinn-i-))

**Interpretation:** The decision is which scene or identification to revisit and whether a theory fits several fragments. One player accumulates an archive; identity, sequence, and context begin incomplete. There is no live opponent fabricating an alibi or player-level cooperation/betrayal. The relevant analogue is testing an account against positions and sequence. Reinterpretation produces surprise, but underlying events are authored. Once answers are known, the game does not preserve repeated-round deduction uncertainty. Ship geography and occupational context organize evidence.

**BAU proposal:** A compact notebook should connect receipts, places, and player claims so people can revise an account. It should distinguish observations from assertions. Do not offer magical scene replays or expose a complete criminal timeline. Generate new mysteries through player action; presentation alone cannot supply replayability.

### Golden Idol rewards causal understanding and intermediate discoveries

**Verified:** The Case of the Golden Idol asks players to reconstruct suspects, motives, and events. Designers describe constrained phrases, smaller sub-puzzles, alternative deduction paths, and avoiding per-word validation because it invites brute force. ([Publisher](https://www.playstack.com/games/goldenidol/), [Designer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/case-of-the-golden-idol))

**Interpretation:** Players decide which contextual clues belong together and whether an explanation is supported. A solo investigator holds discovered clues; sequence and motive remain hidden until interpreted. No participating human manufactures an alibi or betrays another player. Accounts instead compete against physical context. An insight unexpectedly reorganizes the authored story, rather than creating systemic emergence. Fixed solutions limit replay once understood. Belongings, documents, and scene arrangement create its investigative environment.

**BAU proposal:** Allow a small useful deduction—“the box was moved after dispatch”—without resolving guilt. Supply alternative corroboration paths so one unavailable slip does not destroy the mystery. Reject fill-in-the-answer guilt checks and near-correct suspect feedback. Clue readability matters more than accumulating decorative documents.

### Hitman makes the same space support several approaches

**Verified:** IOI documents choices of timing, tools, disguise, overheard information, environmental opportunities, and starting points. Freelancer adds planning, reconnaissance, and independently varied conditions while distinguishing systemic solutions from scripted Mission Stories. ([IOI features](https://ioi.dk/hitman/game-features), [Freelancer design explanation](https://ioi.dk/hitman/blogs/freelancer-you-are-in-control))

**Interpretation:** Preparation competes with improvisation; access competes with exposure. Players possess equipment and discovered knowledge while opportunities and target context require reconnaissance. Behavioral camouflage supplies an alibi against NPC expectations, not human consensus. Cooperation is not the central baseline incentive. Reusable affordances enable unexpected combinations; multiple approaches and changing conditions support experimentation. Buildings, routines, tools, and alternate entrances actively determine possibilities.

**BAU proposal:** Give a returns shelf, parts cabinet, and disposal staging area multiple ordinary uses and observable consequences. Provide two viable routes to a covert transfer without a giant NPC simulation. Reject assassination, identity-replacing disguises, criminal-only shortcuts, scripted guaranteed opportunities, and expansive traversal. A small human coworker network should create the uncertainty.

### Blood on the Clocktower separates sincerity from correctness

**Verified:** Secret characters receive private information; public/private conversation permits concealment. Some good players have reasons to lie. Death preserves discussion participation, while drunkenness/poisoning can make information unreliable. ([Official rules](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Rules_Explanation), [Official states rules](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/States))

**Interpretation:** Players decide when to disclose knowledge and which account to test. Private character results differ; roles and reliability remain hidden. Alibis are consistency among claimed abilities/results rather than movement histories. Pooling observations helps cooperation, while protecting a source or exploiting conflict motivates concealment. Sincere contradictory claims produce surprise. Role combinations and socially negotiated interpretations vary puzzles. Private conversation networks, rather than spatial work, shape the environment.

**BAU proposal:** A truthful witness can misunderstand a parcel; an honest worker can report a late-filed record. Give each conflict a discoverable cause. Preserve participation and innocent reasons to conceal. Do not copy invisible poisoning, arbitrary false logs, storyteller-controlled truth, or a complex role catalog: BAU needs a reconstructable material world.

### Secret Hitler makes hidden pipeline choices contestable

**Verified:** Publicly approved officeholders privately discard policy cards before publishing one outcome. Those discards remain hidden, enabling competing claims about available choices. Investigations reveal party membership rather than the special Hitler identity; execution removes participation. ([Official rules](https://www.secrethitler.com/assets/Secret_Hitler_Rules.pdf))

**Interpretation:** Choosing trusted authority competes with keeping the process moving. Different participants know different pipeline stages; public voters see outcomes but not discarded alternatives. “Those were my options” is an alibi challenged by a partner's account and subsequent conduct. Early cooperation can earn later authority. An honest participant can publish an undesirable outcome, creating unscripted disagreement. Draws, authority rotation, and choices change rounds. The hidden policy pipeline is its environment rather than traversable geography.

**BAU proposal:** Dispatch, temporary custody, and receiving should expose different portions of a parcel's journey. A shortage can originate upstream or downstream; receiving a wrong part does not identify who changed it. Reject allegiance scans, execution, policy tracks, and theme imitation. Checking a disputed transfer should cost work time, not another player's agency.

### The Traitors makes useful teamwork coexist with concealed allegiance

**Verified, limited access:** Official Peacock indexed material describes shared missions, hidden traitors, and protection/recruitment concepts. The full page was region-blocked during research; edition-specific details are not used as universal rules. ([Official format explanation](https://www.peacocktv.com/blog/the-traitors-game-rules))

**Interpretation:** Decisions concern confidants, alliances, and how strongly to echo an accusation. Contestants have personal allegiance and observed relationships; private conversations and motives remain hidden. Viewers' edited privileged knowledge is not contestant evidence. An alibi often rests on shared effort and relationship history, challenged by consistency and commitments. Shared prize-building makes cooperation valuable across conflicting interests. A trusted contributor's concealed objective creates surprise. Cast dynamics generate different stories, but produced television does not establish videogame replay balance. Shared rooms and private conversations control social information.

**BAU proposal:** Hold a short delivery reconciliation where people can volunteer a receipt or request a witnessed handoff. Excellent town work should remain compatible with criminal intent. Reject murder, recruitment, banishment, and body-language guilt systems. Use the format's trust tension without its elimination ceremony.

### Resistance / Avalon makes successful cooperation insufficient proof

**Verified:** Public team approval precedes anonymous quest results; good submits success, while evil can choose success or failure. Avalon adds informed characters such as Merlin; base Resistance advertises no elimination. ([Publisher Avalon page](https://indieboardsandcards.com/our-games/the-resistance-avalon/), [Publisher-authored rules, mirrored PDF](https://cdn.1j1ju.com/medias/a6/dc/c1-the-resistance-avalon-rulebook.pdf), [Resistance publisher page](https://indieboardsandcards.com/our-games/the-resistance/))

**Interpretation:** Players decide whom to trust and when concealment is worth cooperation. Allegiance and some teammate information are private; quest-card authorship remains hidden. Successful participation supports an alibi but cannot establish loyalty, so later outcomes and participants challenge it. Cooperation can buy access to a decisive betrayal. Hidden choices collide unexpectedly. Team histories and negotiation vary rounds; familiar social patterns can still recur. Team constraints and recorded outcomes are its environment.

**BAU proposal:** Remember parcel custodians and let players request voluntary partners. A criminal should make genuine successful deliveries. Do not import formal mission teams, fail cards, or Merlin. At four players, one perfectly isolated two-person failure can collapse ambiguity; retain an honest alternative cause or earlier access window.

### Among Us prices watching against working

**Verified:** Crew complete tasks and investigate through meetings and observation tools; impostors fake tasks, sabotage, and use special movement. Innersloth explicitly discusses preparing alibis. ([Official rules](https://www.innersloth.com/games/among-us/))

**Interpretation:** Staying together offers witnesses but competes with task efficiency. Local sightings and task knowledge are held; intentions, offscreen movement, and testimony remain uncertain. Claimed rooms and tasks create challengeable alibis. Shared task progress motivates cooperation while sabotage redirects attention. Interruptions create unexpected routes and encounters. Changing player decisions refresh rounds, although routine tasks and learned tells can become repetitive. Room connectivity and observation positions structure testimony.

**BAU proposal:** Preserve work-versus-watch decisions and spatial corroboration while replacing fake tasks/kills with useful work/covert custody changes. Keep all four workers through discussion. Avoid criminal-only vents, binary visual innocence tests, continuous colleague trackers, and daily ejection. The mystery should concern what happened to town resources, not who killed a body.

### Goose Goose Duck broadens motives and local conversations

**Verified:** Geese complete tasks and identify ducks; ducks sabotage/eliminate; neutral players have distinct objectives. Proximity voice and varied roles, maps, modes, and tasks are advertised. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1568590/Goose_Goose_Duck/))

**Interpretation:** Players choose companions, disclosure, and challenges. Private goals differ from shared sightings; spoken accounts can be incomplete or misleading. An alibi partly depends on who could see or hear an interaction. Cooperation competes with conflicting objectives. Interruptions and differing purposes create unexpected conversations. Role/map variety changes expectations, though exact information rules vary and were not exhaustively verified. Local distance shapes encounters and witnesses.

**BAU proposal:** Use small personal favors to diversify motives while all innocents remain invested in town work. Human conversation and expressive reactions should enrich actual handling decisions. Avoid standalone neutral victory factions, lethal powers, role bloat, and unrelated minigame collections. Proximity voice is a potential presentation feature, not a prerequisite for testing the core material loop.

### Town of Salem 2 distributes knowledge through asymmetric perspectives

**Verified:** The current developer description presents distinct roles/abilities/objectives, persuasive chat, alliances, concealment, and modes; its advertised player count exceeds the four-player BAU slice. Exact current investigative outputs were not verified. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2140510/Town_of_Salem_2/))

**Interpretation:** Players choose what observation to disclose and whether a claim fits other accounts. Personal ability/objective information differs; identities and intentions remain hidden. Claimed roles and action histories form alibis, challenged through contradictory reports. Different interests make the same public action serve different ends. Colliding private perspectives create surprising testimony; role configurations refresh puzzles but impose learning cost. The retrieved source establishes a social role framework, not a traversable evidence sandbox.

**BAU proposal:** Shop stock, garage diagnosis, and sanitation manifests provide three professional lenses. Let a private favor explain a withheld slip without requiring exhaustive public role claims. Reject a sprawling role catalog, night-kill dependence, direct allegiance results, and forced claim templates. A profession must never identify criminal alignment.

### Schedule I makes buildings and workflows materially useful

**Verified:** Developer descriptions specify production processes, recipes, suppliers/customers, distribution, properties, workers, automation, and cooperative management. They also include combat and police/cartel danger. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3164500/Schedule_I/))

**Interpretation:** Players decide workflow investment, distribution, and allocation. They hold recipe knowledge and observed stock/order state; current activity elsewhere can remain incomplete, but this is not a hidden-allegiance alibi game. Cooperation divides linked duties; betrayal is not its defining baseline. Overlapping production and distribution can generate unscripted coordination. Progression and changing chains sustain repetition beyond one shift. Buildings serve productive functions rather than decoration.

**BAU proposal:** Receiving, processing, handing off, and recording should change real town resources. The same workstation can serve ordinary and covert handling. Reject drugs, empire growth, combat, and automation that eliminates coworker encounters. Precise hand gestures were not verified from the official text; tactile choices below are proposed BAU designs rather than copied specifications.

### PEAK makes help and recoverable mistakes memorable

**Verified:** Four-player climbing includes proximity chat, helping companions, ropes/spikes, and stamina consequences from injury. The island changes daily and can be retried within that day. ([Developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK/))

**Interpretation:** Route and resource choices include deciding who needs assistance. Players see nearby terrain and teammates, while the next safe approach can remain uncertain. Alibis and secret betrayal are not core systems. Cooperation answers real physical obstacles. Mishaps and rescue attempts produce unscripted incidents; different layouts and group abilities vary play. Terrain determines who can help and hear whom.

**BAU proposal:** Optional tray steadying, gate holding, or bolt retrieval should create encounters worth remembering. A spill can redirect traffic and create a witness without deleting scarce stock. Reject lethal falls, survival attrition, forced group travel, and procedural town rebuilding. Use forgiving snapped items and short animation first; do not assume unconstrained network physics will deliver comedy reliably.

## Five areas converge on a causal working town

### Social deception needs legitimate alternatives, not universal doubt

Dale & Dawson offers the clearest work-based precedent; LOCKDOWN Protocol and Project Winter add practical dependence; Avalon shows useful cooperation need not certify loyalty; Clocktower separates sincerity from correctness. **The BAU design implication is an ambiguous purpose attached to a reliable action.** Entering storage should establish access, not guilt. A repair request, ordinary return, and private favor should each justify the visit. Asking for the relevant receipt or destination then narrows the explanation.

Do not make every innocent secret as elaborate as the crime. One worker might hide a damaged component, another a harmless personal pickup, and another an unauthorized loan. Each secret creates a bounded omission or questionable route and can ultimately be explained. The criminal needs a distinct covert objective—divert a required component and conceal its destination—while preserving enough genuine output to operate inside town life. If everyone must constantly sabotage and lie, the meaningful distinction disappears.

### Investigation should compare scopes and reconstruct custody

SpyParty contributes attention limits; Deception contributes explanatory specificity; Obra Dinn contributes organization; Golden Idol contributes intermediate inference and alternative evidence paths. **Proposed evidence should report its own scope clearly.** A count proves a discrepancy, a receipt proves an entry, and an eyewitness supplies an account. Players must decide which additional check distinguishes their hypotheses.

| Proposed evidence | What its holder knows | What remains missing or unreliable | Honest / covert explanations | Useful challenge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Partial receipt | Shop sees category, quantity, package reference, coarse town time | Entry is not a contents inspection; buyer may be omitted | Approved substitution / covered diversion | Compare recipient copy and actual goods |
| Vehicle service/use record | Mechanic sees service interval and job-note revisions | Service does not identify driver, route, or carried goods | Corrected diagnosis / concealed use | Compare feasible timings, key custody, and witnesses; defer actual driving |
| Missing stock | Shop sees opening and present quantity | No automatic identity for remover | Loan or restaging / theft | Check request, shelves, handoffs, and installed parts |
| Package transfer attestation | Giver/receiver know declared reference and destination | Signature certifies a claim, not unseen contents | Helpful relocation / substitution | Inspect seal or contents at an ordinary work cost |
| Sanitation manifest | Collector sees source zone and material band | Batch record does not name every item or previous handler | Repair scrap / concealed component | Compare service output, staging state, and parcel category |
| Witness claim | Worker recalls local presence or handling | Sightline, time estimate, and intent are incomplete | Real errand / selective truth | Ask what they actually saw, then seek a separate trace |
| Corrected service note | Reader sees original, amendment, author, coarse time | Correction motive remains unknown | Honest error / cover-up | Compare physical part and prior request |

This matrix is a **BAU proposal**, not implemented behavior. Missing data must arise from a defined omission, real unrecorded action, or visible document problem; altered data must require a supported player interaction. Never roll invisible false logs. Coarse timestamps must use one shared clock so rounding remains understandable. Two receipts generated by the same event are not independent corroboration: combine a record with physical stock, a different accepted action, or a genuine observation. Do not automatically extract a suspect score, highlight lies, validate theories, or color someone guilty.

### Systemic environments need overlapping purposes and viable routes

Hitman's transferable lesson is recombinable affordances; Schedule I supplies productive infrastructure. **Proposed** storage, alley staging, returns, workshop scrap, and collection points should each support several jobs. A returns shelf moves actual goods, clears working space, exposes a visible parcel, and permits either honest correction or covert rerouting. Objects should have consequences across professions rather than trigger isolated progress bars.

Keep learnable geography and vary dependencies. A request can change destination or priority; a staging entrance can be temporarily unavailable for an explicit ordinary reason. Do not rebuild the town randomly or add unused interiors. The [existing Phase 8 proposal](phase8-living-town-proposal.md) documents a logical warehouse account and useful parts/waste dependencies, but not a playable warehouse interior. Use existing stockroom/locker/alley footprints first. Homes, real vehicle travel, and large NPC routines are later scope.

### Social pressure should change cooperation while preserving participation

The Traitors, Secret Hitler, Avalon, and Town of Salem 2 make trust consequential through commitments and asymmetric knowledge. **Proposed BAU accusations should become actionable requests:** “Will you help inspect this return?”, “Who received your package?”, or “Can you show the amended slip?” Refusing a request creates another question, not a conviction. Partner choice can change who witnesses a delivery; selective disclosure can protect an innocent secret or a criminal plan.

Shared town value should benefit everyone. A criminal who completes an urgent repair can deserve thanks without becoming cleared. Rechecking a package consumes time the investigators also need for work. During the one-day experiment nobody is removed from their job. The [original GDD](../../Working-Town%20Social%20Deduction%20%E2%80%94%20Game%20Design%20Doc.pdf) rejects early daily ejections; stronger surveillance/search/arrest powers belong to a later escalation design, not this first slice. Copying large-group role algebra into four participants would overconstrain the mystery.

### Moment-to-moment fun must survive a quiet investigation

PEAK suggests optional help and recoverable mishaps; Goose Goose Duck suggests expressive local encounters; LOCKDOWN Protocol supplies cooperative urgency; Schedule I supplies linked work. **Proposed work should offer choices before it offers animation.** A shopkeeper chooses customer stock versus a garage request, a mechanic chooses quick diagnosis versus another check, and sanitation chooses collection order versus inspecting an uncertain batch. A short assist improves speed or convenience without becoming mandatory.

A spill should create a new social arrangement rather than erase progress. Keep items conserved in nearby snapped staging, let colleagues help, and leave observations legible. Start with two-slot trays and controlled cart movement instead of ragdolls and arbitrary physics piles. A game that is amusing only during accusation is missing half its identity; the initial work-only control round must stand on its own.

## Adapt interlocking mechanics and reject identity conflicts

### Mechanics worth adapting

All adaptations below are **proposed BAU designs**; references identify their rationale, not identical shipped implementations.

| Mechanic worth adapting | Reference lesson | BAU implementation | Decision generated | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lawful suspicious access | Dale & Dawson, SpyParty | Shared storage, returns, corrections | Work now or invite a witness? | No criminal-only animation/entrance |
| Real productive cover | Avalon, The Traitors | Criminal completes ordinary requests | Earn trust or seize an opening? | Productivity never certifies innocence |
| Material handoff chain | Secret Hitler, Schedule I | Part becomes parcel, installed part, or scrap | Inspect, transfer, substitute, prioritize | No hidden random contents changes |
| Local observation gaps | SpyParty, Among Us | Sightlines and two normal routes | Follow someone or finish a job? | No global colleague tracker |
| Complementary information | Deception, Town of Salem 2 | Stock, service, and collection perspectives | Which coworker can corroborate? | Job is independent of allegiance |
| Organized revisable theories | Obra Dinn, Golden Idol | Minimal notebook with claims/sources | Revisit evidence or defend account? | No answer validation |
| Useful dependence | Project Winter, LOCKDOWN | Supply requests with fallback work | Help, wait briefly, or reprioritize? | No refusal hard-lock |
| Bounded personal motives | Clocktower, Goose Goose Duck | One innocent favor/error per worker | Disclose private context or protect it? | No neutral sabotage faction |
| Optional practical assistance | PEAK | Steady tray, hold gate, retrieve bolt | Help now or continue own task? | No mandatory two-player choke point |
| Constrained round variation | Hitman Freelancer, PEAK | Request/route/priority seed | Reuse knowledge under changed demand | No unconstrained procedural clue noise |

### Mechanics that conflict with the original design

| Conflicting mechanic | Why it conflicts | Preserve the useful principle instead |
|---|---|---|
| Daily firing, banishment, voting people out | Early removal shrinks the working/social town | Voluntary inspections and public commitments |
| Murder/combat as criminal progress | Changes covert working life into elimination | Nonlethal diversion and cover-up |
| Criminal-only vents, hatches, disguises | Route or identity becomes a binary tell | Ordinary alternate routes shared by everyone |
| Allegiance scans or visible innocence checks | Ends causal reasoning with certified alignment | Scoped evidence about actions and objects |
| Randomly false evidence/hidden poisoning | Players cannot reconstruct physical causes | Visible omissions, honest mistakes, supported amendments |
| Huge special-role inventories | Four-player reading becomes role memorization | Three professional perspectives and small private motives |
| Omniscient forensic host, frozen replay, answer slots | Converts live mystery into an authored answer puzzle | Player-created notebook and independent corroboration |
| Full NPC crowds/routines or empire automation | High scope; can displace coworker encounters | Request cards and a few reusable workstations |
| Uncontrolled physics and punitive mishaps | Replication chaos can destroy clue validity | Conserved objects and quick recoveries |
| Repeated unrelated minigames | Adds activity without shared consequence | Tactile actions inside one causal resource chain |

### Ten concrete opportunities to improve Business as Usual

These are design opportunities, not a claim that the systems already exist.

| # | Concrete opportunity | Interlocking benefit | Smallest useful implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Carry one conserved part through dispatch, repair, return, and scrap | Work, custody, and evidence concern the same thing | One part type, two parcels, snapped staging |
| 2 | Make every suspicious location a normal destination for all professions | Presence invites questions without proving guilt | Public request cards with overlapping errands |
| 3 | Give criminal diversion two lawful-looking routes | Criminal improvises rather than repeats a fixed stunt | Market return or garage-scrap collection; both feasible |
| 4 | Give each innocent one bounded secret | Contradictions can be honest, and disclosures matter | Concealed mistake, harmless favor, or unauthorized loan |
| 5 | Distribute complementary partial records | Professionals need each other's context | Receipt, service slip, sanitation manifest |
| 6 | Preserve ordinary correction histories | Changed paperwork supports several motives | One editable note field, old/new value, author/time |
| 7 | Price inspection against actual work | Investigation is a decision rather than constant surveillance | Optional contents check with short handling cost |
| 8 | Add asynchronous shelves and fallback tasks | Assistance is useful without permitting hostage-taking | Visible delivery shelf, independent cleanup/test task |
| 9 | Add one optional assist and recoverable mishap | Quiet shifts still generate remembered encounters | Tray steadying and a controlled nearby spill |
| 10 | Vary a few linked requests and hold a closing reconciliation | Learnable geography supports new stories and revised accounts | Validated seed changes recipient/priority; source-linked notebook |

### Five highest-impact additions, ranked

Fun potential and compatibility use a proposed 1–5 judgment scale, with 5 highest. Effort uses 1–5 with 5 hardest. These are **reasoned planning estimates**, not empirical scores or delivery schedules; effort assumes documented foundations can be reused and must be checked against the current implementation. Rank considers dependencies and identity fit rather than adding scores.

| Rank | Addition | Fun potential | Effort | Compatibility | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conserved object chain with shared access and two covert routes | 5 | 3 | 5 | Makes ordinary handling consequential; everything else needs real custody |
| 2 | Bounded innocent secrets plus genuinely productive criminal cover | 5 | 2 | 5 | Makes the same facts support competing motives without extra role systems |
| 3 | Complementary records and a short reconstruction discussion | 5 | 3 | 5 | Turns suspicion into challengeable reasoning; depends on authoritative events |
| 4 | Choice-rich tactile handling and optional assist | 4 | 3 | 5 | Makes work enjoyable between accusations; controlled presentation limits scope |
| 5 | Validated request/priority variation and one ordinary incident | 4 | 2 | 5 | Changes encounters cheaply after routes and evidence are coherent |

Free physics, carts, four-client reliability, and voice infrastructure can raise effort substantially. Do not hide those dependencies inside an optimistic content estimate. A beautiful tray interaction is lower priority than a conserved, interpretable transfer, but a choice-free transfer is not enough to pass the work-fun gate.

## Three scenarios show systems generating competing explanations

These are **possible emergent outcomes in proposed systems**, not scripted accusations or playtest results. Initial requests and incentives are seeded; player timing, cooperation, routes, and disclosure decide what happens.

### A late return connects two unrelated secrets

A shopkeeper dispatches a replacement. An innocent mechanic realizes an earlier diagnosis was wrong, temporarily borrows the parcel for a check, and amends the service note to avoid embarrassment. The assistant sees a worker carrying a sealed box toward alley staging but never sees contents. Meanwhile the criminal directs a different, similar parcel through the ordinary returns route and diverts its component.

At closing, missing stock and the mechanic's correction support an attractive but incomplete accusation. Comparing package references, the installed component, and the sanitation batch separates the two incidents. The mechanic can disclose the mistake or allow suspicion to persist; the criminal can truthfully describe useful work while omitting the return. A correction establishes alteration, not intent. If one record immediately names the criminal, the evidence scope is too broad; if neither parcel can be distinguished at all, there is insufficient corroboration.

### A helpful spill creates witnesses without destroying truth

Sanitation's tray catches a curb and moves its bags into nearby recovery slots. Two coworkers help. An innocent shopkeeper takes back a reusable carton needed for a private favor. The criminal uses the normal restaging opportunity to place a diverted component into a workshop-scrap parcel.

One witness remembers the shopkeeper holding packaging; another remembers the criminal helping. Both can be sincere. The manifest identifies a source zone and material band, not the full contents. Inspecting the parcel and comparing the repair's expected waste can test the accounts. Accepted custody remains correct internally; no physics bug deletes an item or becomes a false clue. The interaction can be funny even if nobody notices the covert step, because helping restores productive work quickly.

### Excellent work buys trust for a questionable favor

An urgent garage request competes with customer orders. The criminal completes a useful delivery and asks an innocent sanitation assistant to carry a “return” while finishing another task. The assistant agrees, but conceals a separate personal pickup that delayed their route. Town Stability benefits from genuine completed work.

Later, the return is missing a component. The helper can establish custody without knowing purpose; the criminal's productivity cannot clear them. Comparing dispatch quantity, recipient state, and the collection interval reveals an unexplained access window. Players can request a joint inspection next time rather than eject the helper. The dilemma is whom to trust with another delivery while work still matters, not whether useful contribution mechanically proves innocence.

## A four-player experiment should earn the larger Phase 8 plan

### Use the existing three professions and a rotated assistant

The [original design](../../Working-Town%20Social%20Deduction%20%E2%80%94%20Game%20Design%20Doc.pdf) sets the full concept at six players/five days and an initial MVP at four players/three days. This research recommends a **one-day diagnostic slice within that progression**, not replacing either target. Use Shopkeeper, Mechanic, and Sanitation, with the fourth player sharing one profession as a shift assistant. Give the assistant distinct dispatch, intake, or collection responsibilities and a partial perspective; do not duplicate the whole job or add a detective/courier profession. Rotate the shared profession and assign criminal status independently. Test whether the assistant stays useful and whether sharing information makes one job an accidental innocence certificate.

Use a 60–90-second briefing, a configurable **7–10-minute workday**, a 3–4-minute closing discussion, and a short private explanation/debrief. The existing documented 600-second ambient visual cycle is not evidence of a functioning gameplay timer. No Day 1 ejection, arrest, or forced confession is needed. At closing, each participant submits a private theory naming the item, likely chain, and supporting observations before full truth is revealed. This measures reasoning; it is not an automatic in-world guilt checker. A final accusation win condition belongs in the later three-day MVP; five-day escalation remains a separate full-design question.

### Sequence implementation around one playable causal loop

**Stage A: verify the reusable foundation.** Confirm four-client session setup, role assignment, accepted inventory transfers, join/reconnect behavior, and private/public information boundaries. Existing [Phase 8 documentation](phase8-living-town-proposal.md) describes useful authoritative parts/waste dependencies and presentation separation, but its prior two-process checks do not establish four-human social play or LAN comfort. Run the work loop first without secrets or a criminal. Give each profession two actual choices and useful fallback work.

**Stage B: add the minimum deduction triangle.** Connect receipt, service slip, and collection manifest to the same accepted object events. Add selective contents inspection and a minimal player-authored notebook. Teach each record's omissions. Ordinary amendments preserve earlier entries. Keep a complete private developer audit for correctness/debrief, separate from the in-world evidence view.

**Stage C: introduce motives and routes together.** Add one nonlethal diversion objective, one secret per innocent, and two routes: approved market return and workshop-scrap collection. Both need normal job traffic, at least one opportunity without deliberate work failure, and independent corroboration. Validate seeded request combinations before play so neither route becomes impossible or uniquely criminal. Begin with selective disclosure and one note amendment; full forgery, document damage, NPC witnesses, real driving, and complex searches are non-goals.

For the first build, define covert completion precisely: the designated component must reach the returns hold or scrap holding cage outside legitimate repair custody before closing, and the criminal must complete at least one ordinary request. Both destinations are reachable through lawful errands by every profession. Maintain a replacement in the municipal reserve so the repair can still succeed; reserve use costs time, not an impossible dependency. The server evaluates objective state privately at closing. No completion popup tells colleagues that a crime occurred or identifies its actor. Whether the criminal maintained a believable account is a human playtest observation, not a lie-detection rule.

Use a deliberately small proposed Town Stability rule during tests: start at 5, add 1 for each of three priority requests completed, and subtract 1 for each left unfinished at closing; cap at 0–8. Wrong diagnoses, spills, and reserve collection impose no direct score penalty. The criminal values stability through their required legitimate contribution, while innocents share the town result. Report output and missing-stock discrepancies separately, so an inventory shortage cannot mechanically certify criminal intent. Keep these tunable values fixed within a comparison block and record changes between sessions.

**Stage D: add bounded feel and variation.** Add one optional assist, one controlled recoverable spill, and recipient/priority variation. Recheck that comedy neither destroys inventory nor obscures every clue. Only expand locations or professions after the integrated slice passes. This strengthens the existing implementation sequencing by introducing work, motive, and evidence early enough to evaluate their interaction.

### Keep authoritative truth private and player evidence scoped

The following is a proposed boundary inside the existing authority/presentation approach, not a replacement architecture. Each accepted event should retain an internal event ID, item ID, actor, action, source/destination custody, authoritative time/revision, and relevant prior/new state. Object conservation and permissions are server responsibilities. Reject stale revisions and duplicate transfers; recover snapshots without duplicating parcels or losing amendments.

Generate in-world records as explicit projections: record ID/type, package/category link where appropriate, visible fields, time granularity, author/attestation, revision history, and access/disclosure policy. A receipt need not expose actor identity; a service note can expose its editor without exposing intent. Player testimony is an attributed claim, never a verified event. Criminal objective and innocent secret data go only to the owning player and authorized server logic. Public productivity, remote UI, snapshots, logs, and spectator/debug tools must not leak them. Test with client inspection and reconnects before interpreting accusations as meaningful social behavior. Complete debug truth stays outside play and is revealed only in debrief.

### Run repeated sessions with fair rotation and falsifiable gates

Recruit four active human players; automated clients establish synchronization, not enjoyment. Run **ten rounds across three sessions**, allowing breaks. The two controls have no criminal; the eight integrated rounds give every participant criminal status twice through an explicit schedule. Rotate assistant/profession assignments independently and balance each participant's exposure to both covert routes. Explain record limits once, then check comprehension without revealing strategy.

Round 1 is work-only; round 2 uses the same loop with secrets but no criminal to test explainable innocent suspicion. Rounds 3–8 add the criminal and records, alternate preferred route, and change only request/priority seeds. Rounds 9–10 compare the best integrated variant with one bounded feature removed—for example, optional assistance or the notebook—using matched seeds and alternating order across groups. Do not remove every record and call the resulting confusion evidence of difficulty. Small-group learning and familiarity confound comparisons; retain qualitative accounts and avoid statistical claims from one group.

The following proposed rotation uses participant labels A–D. Assign the three remaining workers to the unshared lead jobs with balanced rotations. Assistant and criminal assignments deliberately overlap in some rounds and differ in others. The listed route is the route to exercise for coverage, not secret exclusive access; both remain available.

| Proposed gate | Measurement | Initial pass condition | Response to failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Causal/network validity | Private audit versus accepted inventory and visible records | Zero duplicate/lost objects or private-objective leaks; all records explainable | Fix correctness before evaluating deduction |
| Useful work for everyone | Observer samples every 30 seconds; task/choice logs | Every player makes two consequential work choices; nobody involuntarily blocked over 60 seconds | Improve independent work and reserve fallback |
| Work-only enjoyment | Private 1–5 rating and memorable-event account | At least 3 of 4 rate work 4+ and describe a decision or voluntary interaction | Revise handling/choices before adding more clues |
| Two viable covert routes | Route audit and criminal's private account | Each route remains feasible in every tested seed without criminal-only access | Repair seed constraints or legal overlap |
| Explainable suspicion | Pre-reveal theories and source references | At least one innocent action has a concrete innocent explanation; at least two players contribute distinct evidence | Tune secret scope and evidence distribution |
| Reasoning improves an account | Discussion coding and private before/after theory | At least one account is revised through corroboration in most integrated rounds | Add discriminating context; simplify notebook/record wording |
| No deterministic single clue | Record review and first suspicions | No UI/animation/profession uniquely certifies guilt; conclusions use multiple perspectives | Reduce source scope, not truthfulness |
| Replay holds attention | Repeat-round ratings, remembered incidents, route choices | At least 3 of 4 want another round after session; changed seeds produce materially different encounters | Change dependencies/access, not decorative item counts |

These thresholds are first-pass design gates, not industry benchmarks or invented results. Record time spent working, walking, waiting, talking, and observing; voluntary assists; abandoned requests; culprit's genuine output; evidence-backed versus unsupported accusations; alternative accounts; and perceived arbitrariness. Ask privately what each person saw, what they inferred, confidence, and what would change their mind before revealing ground truth. Do not optimize a target false-accusation rate: both immediate certainty and indiscriminate distrust can be failures.

If all players identify the criminal from route choice, add ordinary traffic or a lawful reason to use it. If every clue fits every story, add a discriminating package reference, condition, or sequence relationship. If work is abandoned for surveillance, adjust workload and Town Stability incentives rather than banning observation. If secrets are disclosed instantly without a dilemma, strengthen a small personal incentive without making confession catastrophic. If spills dominate recollection but no one can reconstruct custody, reduce comedy's obstruction. Advance to the three-day MVP only when players can both enjoy work and explain how evidence changed a belief.
