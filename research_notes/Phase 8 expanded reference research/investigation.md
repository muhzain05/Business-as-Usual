# Investigation references for Phase 8

Evidence status: **Verified** means supported by the linked publisher, developer manual, or first-person developer interview. **Inference** is design interpretation, not an empirically demonstrated player outcome. **Proposal** is a new Business as Usual design, not a reference-game mechanic. Research checked 12 October 2026. No popularity or causal fun claims are inferred from sales or review scores.

## How do the four references create satisfying investigation?

### Takeaway

The transferable principle is evidence interpretation: players enjoy deciding which observation matters and reconciling facts that initially support several explanations. BAU should distribute those observations through ordinary work; it should not borrow the references' sniper execution, murder framing, frozen-world structure, or answer-validation machinery.

### Cited Findings

#### SpyParty — verified foundation

- SpyParty's core is asymmetric: a spy completes missions while blending into party guests; a sniper observes and has one bullet. Behavior and perception, rather than action combat, are the stated focus. [Developer homepage](https://www.spyparty.com/)
- The developer's manual distinguishes visible action tells from behavior without a hard tell. Examples include a statue changing, microfilm handling or a book returned to another bookcase, repeated proximity to a target, and inspecting statues. Carrying a drink blocks some actions but provides social cover. The spy sees the sniper's laser; occlusion and observation angle matter. The manual advises watching purposeful behavior as well as specific animations. [Developer manual, especially pp. 2–4](https://shared.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/329070/manuals/SpyParty-Manual-screen.pdf?t=1726041201)
- The developer explains that allowing a subset of missions stops the observer from simply camping a mandatory action and forces attention allocation under uncertainty. [Developer archive containing mission-subsetting explanation](https://www.spyparty.com/page/23/?orderemail=&payment=)
- Beginner training teaches both spy and sniper perspectives for four missions, illustrating that observation rules are learnable rather than completely inscrutable. [Developer tutorial post](https://www.spyparty.com/2018/02/28/the-new-spyparty-tutorial/)

#### Deception: Murder in Hong Kong — verified foundation

- Roles and sides are hidden. A murderer chooses a means and key evidence; the forensic scientist knows the solution but communicates through scene tiles without verbal or gestural hints. Investigators interpret those clues while the murderer misdirects. Merely naming the murderer is insufficient: investigators must identify both objects. The optional witness and accomplice are for six or more players, so they are not part of its four-player baseline. [Publisher's explanation of roles](https://greyfoxgames.com/collections/current-games/products/deception-murder-in-hong-kong)
- The publisher lists 4–12 players and links the English rulebook. The rules are the appropriate authority for dealing, clue rounds, and formal guesses; avoid substituting an unofficial summary. [Publisher rules entry](https://greyfoxgames.com/collections/deception-murder-in-hong-kong-1); [Official English rulebook](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0669/3961/7510/files/deception_rulebook_english.pdf?v=1699426602)

#### Return of the Obra Dinn — verified foundation

- The player is an insurance investigator examining an abandoned merchant ship; the official description calls the game exploration and logical deduction, with deaths depicted in freeze-frame scenes. [Developer storefront description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/653530/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn/); [Official site](https://obradinn.com/)
- Lucas Pope describes organizing deaths both chronologically and in discovery order, making each scene interesting independently, and giving players time to settle into the loop. A book was added to handle narrative complexity: it organizes out-of-order events and gives a rationale for recording identities and fates. His previous simpler pages included a muster, sea chart, and deck map. The design goal was identities and fates rather than compulsory comprehension of every larger story detail. [First-person developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-lucas-pope-s-i-return-of-the-obra-dinn-i-)

#### The Case of the Golden Idol — verified foundation

- The official description asks players to investigate scenes, construct theories, and deduce suspects, motives, and weapons across connected deaths. [Publisher page](https://www.playstack.com/games/goldenidol/)
- The designers describe a constrained phrase interface; identity and scenario-specific sub-puzzles provide intermediate progress. They rejected validating every word slot because it enables brute force, but used a near-correct indicator to mitigate frustration. They reduced incidental information to necessary clues or useful misdirection, sometimes supported several routes to the same deduction, and removed an explicit will that made an inheritance problem trivial. They distinguish identifying a culprit from understanding an initially confusing situation. [First-person designer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/case-of-the-golden-idol)

### Inferences

### Gaps

- These sources establish rules and developer intent, not controlled evidence that a given mechanic increases enjoyment in a four-player BAU session. All fun predictions require playtesting.
- No primary source inspected establishes an appropriate clue density, round length, or balance for BAU. The experiment below is a proposed local test.
- The old SpyParty manual includes historical implementations and experimental missions; use it for its documented observation/cover principles, not a claim about every mission's current availability.

## How should partial workplace evidence support reasoning without guilt indicators?

### Takeaway

**Proposal:** separate physical traces, generated records, and player assertions. Each should have a legible scope: a stock count reports quantity, a signed receipt reports an attestation, and an eyewitness reports only what they saw. None should say who is guilty.

### Cited Findings

- SpyParty's developer manual supplies the contrast between observable actions and behavior without a hard tell; its book-placement example shows a changed object state functioning as evidence. [Manual](https://shared.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/329070/manuals/SpyParty-Manual-screen.pdf?t=1726041201)
- Golden Idol's designers explicitly balance clue sufficiency against trivially revealing the answer, and describe multiple deduction routes. [Designer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/case-of-the-golden-idol)

### Inferences

**Proposed design, all rows:** three everyday job perspectives should overlap without reproducing the same complete evidence. Assign criminal alignment independently of profession; one criminal must still work, so a profession cannot become a role tell. Exact roster integration remains for the coordinator to align with the existing Phase 8 plan.

| Record or trace | What each relevant job sees | Limits explicitly communicated to players | Innocent explanation / criminal explanation | Corroboration action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Partial receipt | Shop worker sees till copy: item category, quantity, total, issuer; courier sees delivery slip: destination, package reference; mechanic sees parts request. | Receipt records what was entered/signed, not a scanner's complete contents inspection. A torn edge is visible and never silently changes text. Time displayed at a documented coarse resolution. | Innocent: substitute item or personal purchase hidden from coworkers. Criminal: legitimate purchase covers a swapped or diverted package. | Match copies by reference; inspect actual goods; ask recipient. Matching signatures establish a claim, not authenticity of contents. |
| Vehicle service log | Mechanic sees issued vehicle/key, service entries and correction history; driver sees own trip and fuel/odometer state; shop sees arrival and loading. | Odometer establishes distance, not route or driver; a key checkout establishes custody, not continuous driving. A manual destination is a claim. Corrections remain revisions rather than silently replacing original data. | Innocent: road closure, errand, correcting an accidental reading. Criminal: covert detour or altered explanation for distance. | Compare distance with feasible route, witness arrival, work completion, and package custody. Several people may use one vehicle. |
| Missing goods | Shop sees opening quantity and current count; courier sees loaded consignments; mechanic sees requisitioned part. | Inventory decreases on actual removal; the engine never randomly lies about count. It does not identify remover. An authorized loan may be entered later but is visible as later entry. | Innocent: borrowed repair part, dropped box, mistaken shelf, delayed paperwork. Criminal: theft/diversion or false loan story. | Search transit/staging shelves; compare request, handoff, and delivered quantity; reconcile discrepancies while continuing work. |
| Package handoff | Giver sees label and where placed; receiver sees visible seal and signed delivery claim; nearby worker sees an external box. | A handoff record means that named players confirmed transfer, not that contents were inspected. Seal tracks opening only if this interaction actually occurs; identical outer packaging does not imply identical contents. | Innocent: colleague moved it out of the rain or completed a favor. Criminal: substitution during legitimate handling. | Inspect seal/contents with practical cost; compare recorded reference and destination; ask why staging location changed. |
| Witness observation | Any player sees the local interaction within sightline and hearing range; a job gives a reason to be there. | No omniscient NPC identifying perpetrator. Prototype uses actual player perception. Notebook captures a player's typed claim and author, not a certified fact. | Innocent: remembered coat/box but wrong individual; private errand. Criminal: selective truth, concealed step, knowingly false account. | Ask when, where, what object, visibility, and whether they saw possession or only presence. Compare independent perspectives, allowing honest uncertainty. |
| Conflicting account | Each player has own timeline plus selected disclosed work records. | Display contradictions between quoted statements only if player requests comparison; no lie detector, confidence percentage, suspect score, or colored guilt marker. | Innocent: rounded times, different clock basis, mistaken transfer assumption. Criminal: impossible story or altered paperwork. | Test travel feasibility and sequence; obtain another record; state what remains unresolved. |

Implementation semantics: **missing** means a real unrecorded action or a visibly unavailable/damaged document; **forged** means a player performed a supported alteration action with defined access and opportunity. Do not generate false logs by random dice roll. Preserve authoritative action history privately for debugging/debrief, but never expose a complete forensic timeline during play. The player's edited document may overwrite visible content if the mechanic is explicitly supported, while detectable physical/revision traces should be ordinary and ambiguous (e.g., corrected quantity), not a special criminal stamp.

Evidence should narrow a claim, not a person by itself: a changed service entry establishes alteration; it does not establish intent. A missing package establishes discrepancy; it does not distinguish theft from relocation. A witness places a worker in a warehouse; a work order may explain why. The deduction is the intersection of records, physical state, opportunity, and accounts. Multiple independent paths must exist so destroying one receipt does not make a criminal unknowable.

Four-player design hazard: if everyone can instantly see everything, the criminal has nowhere to create uncertainty; if all documents are opaque, accusations become personality guessing. Stage physical access and work interruptions, allow players to voluntarily disclose records, and reserve only one or two deliberate record limitations per source. Do not make every receipt corrupted or every witness unreliable. A stable town clock and coarse displayed times avoid accidental network timing becoming an intended clue.

### Gaps

- The evidence matrix is a proposal. It requires implementation inspection, synchronization testing, and playtests before being described as current BAU behavior.
- Full document forgery, persistent handwriting comparison, NPC witness simulation, or physical paper damage may exceed the one-day prototype. Start with custody attestations and editable job notes with visible revision history; test deception through selective disclosure first.

## What prototype would test these principles with four players?

### Takeaway

**Proposal:** test a small working economy with an evidence triangle, not a complete detective simulation. Four players should have consequential work, a bounded nonlethal covert objective, and enough legitimate private motives that the same suspicious interaction can be honest or criminal.

### Cited Findings

- Deception requires explaining selected evidence as well as identifying a suspect; its optional informed roles begin at six players. [Publisher](https://greyfoxgames.com/collections/current-games/products/deception-murder-in-hong-kong)
- Obra Dinn's developer used familiar book conventions to organize complex observations. [Developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-lucas-pope-s-i-return-of-the-obra-dinn-i-)

### Inferences

**Proposal:** make one parcel loop connecting shop/warehouse, courier, and mechanic. Give the fourth player a working generalist/assistant position that shares any of those jobs rather than adding a detective profession. Align this with the existing roster; maintain only three profession systems. Every worker receives one legitimate private motive such as quietly replacing a damaged part, concealing a personal pickup, or doing an unauthorized but harmless favor. The criminal receives a nonlethal diversion objective and must maintain ordinary output. Use only one innocent secret per player initially; secrets should explain behavior, not deliberately sabotage all evidence.

**Evidence triangle:** shop stock/till note + delivery attestation + mechanic distance/service note. Interactions include carry/place/handoff/inspect, key borrow/return, complete job, and write/correct note. A correction remains attributable to the note editor but not automatically criminal. A borrowed vehicle and real work order can support either cover or honest help. One blocked loading entrance creates a legitimate alternative route; ensure all players know the blockage.

**One test scenario:** shop worker privately damaged a replacement part and orders another; mechanic corrects a service record after borrowing the van to collect it; courier sees the mechanic in the warehouse carrying an unmarked box. During the same work loop, a criminal assistant uses a legitimate staging transfer to divert a different package. At closing, missing stock and increased van mileage tempt players to conflate two incidents. A receipt copy and signed handoff can separate them, if someone chooses to ask. This is a test setup, not a scripted accusation: players determine timing, route, disclosure, and handling.

**Experiment:** run four short rounds with profession rotation; first explain record limits and let players complete the work loop without criminal incentives. Then play with one criminal, one secret each, and end-of-day reconstruction; repeat the same core loop with changed destination, private motive, and criminal assignment. Compare with one round where package transfer uses the same physical loop but records are absent. Do not treat four sessions as statistically conclusive. Record whether people can explain what evidence means, whether accusations cite at least two independent observations, whether players repair an initially wrong theory, how much useful work finishes, and whether the criminal has at least two believable opportunities.

**Suggested acceptance criteria, proposals:** every player makes at least one consequential work decision; at least two players contribute distinct evidence; at least one innocent action creates explainable suspicion; at least one suspicion is reduced by corroboration; nobody receives guilt from an automated UI; criminal can act without deliberately failing their whole job; players can describe the package's plausible custody chain at the end. Failure examples: all players camp the warehouse, criminal is identified solely by profession/animation, the notebook solves the mystery, or a single destroyed slip removes every deduction path.

**Scope ranking for coordinator:** (1) real package custody and ordinary work orders; (2) partial but trustworthy job records; (3) small innocent secrets tied to the same interactions; (4) a player-authored notebook distinguishing observed state from claims; (5) alternate route/key borrowing. Test before undertaking elaborate forgery, NPC behavioral modeling, or automatic contradiction extraction.

### Gaps

- Engineering effort is qualitative until repository inspection verifies existing interaction, inventory, logging, UI, and networking infrastructure. No implementation hours are asserted here.
- The proposed session outcomes and thresholds are test hypotheses, not verified research findings.
